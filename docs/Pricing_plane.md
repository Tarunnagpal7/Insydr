## 🧠 Profitability Strategy

### 1. "Remove Branding" = #1 Conversion Driver
Indian business owners **hate** having another company's badge on their site. This alone converts Free → Starter at ₹799.

### 2. Lead Generation = ROI Justification
D2C and e-commerce businesses in India spend ₹5-50 per lead on Meta/Google ads. If Insydr captures even 100 leads/month, the ₹2,499 Growth plan pays for itself instantly. **This is the upsell story.**

### 3. Annual Billing Push
17% discount on annual plans. Critical for Indian buyers who are deal-seeking. Improves cash flow and reduces churn.

### 4. Usage-Based Overage via Razorpay/Stripe
Auto-charge overages when users hit message limits. Start with Razorpay for Indian cards/UPI, Stripe for international.

### 5. Start Low, Increase Later
Launch at these aggressive prices. Once you have 200+ paying users and proven value, increase Starter to ₹999 and Growth to ₹2,999. Indian users accept 10-20% price hikes if value is proven.

### 6. Freemium Virality
Every free user's widget shows "Powered by Insydr" — free marketing. Target 1,000 free users → 70 conversions to Starter/Growth (7% conversion, standard for Indian PLG SaaS).
